Step 1 Google Sheet & Gemini API

Your 5-tab Google Sheet is already created. Grab a free Gemini API key from Google AI Studio (no card needed) for smart categorization.

1. Open Google AI Studio API Keys ↗ and click Create API Key. 2. In your Google Sheet, click Extensions → Apps Script and paste telegram_expense_bot_apps_script.js.
Open the sheet
Step 2 Telegram bot (11:00 AM Check-In)

Checks in at 11:00 AM every day and asks for yesterday's spends, in the same shorthand as the log bar.

1. Message @BotFather ↗ on Telegram with /newbot and copy your TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN. 2. Paste your Bot Token and Gemini Key at the top of CONFIG in Apps Script. 3. Message your new bot /start once deployed so it auto-captures your Chat ID.
Step 3 GitHub Pages & Live Sync

Hosts this page for free and connects directly to your Apps Script Web App endpoint.

1. In Apps Script, click Deploy → New deployment → Web app (Execute as: Me, Access: Anyone) and copy the /exec URL. 2. Paste that URL into setupEverything() in Apps Script and click Run once. 3. Push index.html and README.md to GitHub, enable Settings → Pages, and paste your /exec URL above.
